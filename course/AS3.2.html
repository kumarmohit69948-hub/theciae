Syllabus lecture plan

  1. 01Magnetism: Dia, para and ferro-magnetism- classification
  2. 02Langrevin's theory of dia, and para magnetism, adiabatic demagnetization
  3. 03Weiss molecular field theory
  4. 05Time dependent and time independent Schrodinger equation
  5. 06Spectroscopy: Qualitative explanation of Zeeman effect, Stark effect and
  6. 09Superconductivity; critical magnetic field, Meissner effect, isotope effect
  7. 10Josephson's effect, DC and AC squids, introduction to high Tc 1 superconductors
  8. 11LASERS and MASERS: Spontaneous and stimulated emission, Einstein A 2 and B coefficients, population inversion
  9. 12He, Ne and Ruby lasers
  10. 13Ammonia and Ruby masers
  11. 14Holography and optical fiber: optical fiber- physical structure, basic theory, 2 type of modes
  12. 16Illumination: laws of illumination, luminous flux, luminous intensity, candle 2 power, brightness
