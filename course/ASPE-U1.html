Syllabus topic plan

  4. 04Modes of heat transfer; heat exchangers
  5. 05Mass transfer and mass-heat-momentum transfer analogies
  6. 06Fluid statics and fluid dynamics; continuity equation and Bernoulli's theorem
  7. 07Dimensional analysis and simulation
  8. 08Simulation models and mathematical modelling
  9. 09Finite difference and finite element analysis
