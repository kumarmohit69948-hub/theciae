Farm Structures, Farmstead Planning and Plant Design

ASPE-U2 Agricultural Structures & Process Engineering

Open notes & files in the library → ▶ Study reels

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Farmstead planning: survey, data collection, analysis of data and layouts
  2. 02Cost estimation, appraisal and project development
  3. 03Time, motion and input analysis; flow charts, drawings and case studies
  4. 04Farm electrification: load estimation and selection of equipment
  5. 05Standby power units: selection, maintenance and operation
  6. 06Farm fencing and safety devices including fire-fighting
  7. 07Benefit-cost analysis; household electrical wiring
  8. 08Illumination, transmission and distribution of electricity
  9. 09Rural water supply and sanitary structures; septic tank design
  10. 10Livestock housing: poultry, piggery and other animal house structures; environment control
  11. 11Rural road culverts and nallah structures
  12. 12Rat-proof godowns and feed stuff storage structures
  13. 13Farm workshop, machinery and implement sheds; biogas plant
  14. 14Greenhouse construction, operation and maintenance; economics of greenhouse production
  15. 15Plant size, product and process design; process flow charts
  16. 16Analysis of plant cost and profitability; preparation and appraisal of project report
