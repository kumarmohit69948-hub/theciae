Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01EMC, sorption and desorption isotherms; water activity and psychrometry
  2. 02Storage environment and its interaction with stored product
  3. 03Factors influencing shelf life; quality deterioration mechanisms and their control
  4. 04Storage practices for food grains including fumigation
  5. 05Design of bulk storage and aeration systems
  6. 06Analysis of heat, moisture and gas transfer in bulk storage structures
  7. 07Bag storage structures: design and management
  8. 08Storage of perishables: ventilated, refrigerated, controlled and modified atmosphere systems
  9. 09Quality analysis of stored produce
  10. 10Storage structures for animal feed and silage
  11. 11Chilling rooms and walk-in cooling rooms for perishables
  12. 12BIS standards for grain storage systems; CAP storage and hermetic storage
