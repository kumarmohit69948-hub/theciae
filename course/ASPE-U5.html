Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Grading, cleaning, washing and sorting

  7. 07Evaporation and distillation
  8. 08Mixing, clarification and coagulation
  9. 09Mechanical separation and sedimentation
  10. 10Screw pressing/expelling, leaching and extraction
  11. 11Pelleting, extrusion and industrial fermentation
