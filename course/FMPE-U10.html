Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Importance of precision agriculture; mapping in farming for decision-making
  2. 02Geographical concepts of precision agriculture; understanding and identifying variability
  3. 03GPS basics: space, receiver and control segments; error and correction; function and usage
  4. 04GIS: functions and use for decision-making
  5. 05Yield monitors and variable rate applicators for fertilizers, seed and chemicals
  6. 06Remote sensing: aerial and satellite imagery; above-ground non-contact sensors
  7. 07Data analysis concepts: resolution and surface analysis
  8. 08Interpretive products: maps, charts and application maps
  9. 09Electronics and control systems for variable rate applications; precision variable equipment
  10. 10Tractor-implement interface technology
  11. 11Environmental implications of precision agriculture; spatial analysis and variable rate application
