Engines and Tractor Systems

FMPE-U4 Farm Machinery & Power Engineering

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Engineering thermodynamics, power cycles and fuels
  2. 02Various systems of IC engines: operation, adjustment and troubleshooting
  3. 03Calculations of power, torque, speed; firing arrangement and intervals
  4. 04Heat load and power transmission from piston to the flywheel
  5. 05Tractor power transmission: clutch, gearbox, differential and final drives
  6. 06Power outlets: P.T.O. and drawbar
  7. 07Recent trends in tractor design; emissions and control of pollutants
  8. 08Mechanical and power steering
  9. 09Tractor chassis mechanics and hitching systems
  10. 10Hydraulic controls for tractors; automatic position and draft control
  11. 11Tractor performance tests
  12. 12Operation and maintenance of tractors and power tillers
