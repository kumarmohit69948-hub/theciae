B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering · Semester 5

Tractor and Automotive Engines

FMPE5.4 Farm Machinery & Power Engineering

Open notes & files in the library → ▶ Study reels

Syllabus lecture plan

  1. 01Sources of farm power: conventional and non-conventional energy sources
  2. 02Classification of tractors and IC engines
  3. 03Review of thermodynamic principles of IC (CI and SI) engines and deviation 2 from ideal cycle; General energy equation and heat balance sheet
  4. 04Derivation of thermal efficiency of Otto cycle, Diesel cycle and Dual cycle
  5. 06Engine strokes and comparison of 2-stroke and 4-stroke engine cycles and CI 1 and SI engines
  6. 07Engine valve systems, valve mechanism, valve timing diagram, valve 3 clearance adjustment; Cam profile, valve lift and valve opening area
  7. 08Inlet and exhaust systems; Importance of air cleaning system; Types of air 2 cleaners and performance characteristics of various air cleaners
  8. 09Fuel supply system, types of fuels, properties of fuels, calculation of air-fuel 4 ratio. Different tests on fuel for SI and CI engines; Detonation and knocking in IC engines; Carburetion system, carburetors and their main functional components; Fuel injection system- injection pump, their types, working principles; Fuel injector nozzles- types and working principles
  9. 10Engine governing- need of governors, governor types and governor 2 characteristics
  10. 11Lubrication system- need, types, functional components; Lubricants- physical 3 properties, additives and their application
  11. 12Engine cooling system- need, cooling methods and main functional 3 components; Need and types of thermostat valves; Additives in the coolant
  12. 13Ignition system of SI engines; Electrical system including battery, starting 3 motor, battery charging, cut-out, etc.; Comparison of dynamo and alternator
  13. 14Basics of engine testing
