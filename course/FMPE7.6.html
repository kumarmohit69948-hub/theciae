Machine Design

FMPE7.6 Farm Machinery & Power Engineering

Syllabus lecture plan

  1. 01Phases of design, design considerations
  2. 02Common engineering materials and their mechanical properties
  3. 03Types of loads and stresses
  4. 04Theories of failure, factor of safety, selection of allowable stress
  5. 06Design of shafts under torsion and combined bending and torsion
  6. 07Design of keys; Design of muff, sleeve, and rigid flange couplings
  7. 08Cotter joints, design of socket and spigot cotter joint; knuckle joint
  8. 09Design of welded subjected to static loads
  9. 10Design of helical and leaf springs
  10. 11Design of threaded fasteners subjected to direct static loads, bolted joints 3 loaded in shear and bolted joints subjected to eccentric loading
  11. 12Design of flat belt drives and pulleys
  12. 13Design of V-belt drives and pulleys
  13. 14Design of gears
