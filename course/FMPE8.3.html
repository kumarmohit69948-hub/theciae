  7. 07Off-road traction and mobility, traction model, traction improvement, tyres- 3 functions, size, lug geometry and their effects
  8. 08Tyre selection and testing
  9. 09Soil compaction and plant growth and variability
  10. 10Application of GIS in soil dynamics
