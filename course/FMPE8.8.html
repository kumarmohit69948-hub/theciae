  9. 09Introduction to sensors and application of sensors for data generation
  10. 10Database management; System concept, system approach in farm machinery 3 management
  11. 11Problems on machinery selection
  12. 12Maintenance and scheduling of operations
  13. 13Application of PERT and CPM in machinery system management
