Design of Surface Irrigation Systems

IDE-501 Irrigation & Drainage Engineering

Open notes & files in the library → ▶ Study reels

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Climate and irrigation water requirement
  2. 02Irrigation principles: losses, conveyance, distribution, application and water budgeting
  3. 03Estimation of effective rainfall
  4. 04Irrigation software: CROPWAT and AQUACROP
  5. 05Farm irrigation systems and irrigation efficiencies
  6. 06Economic feasibility of irrigation methods
  7. 07Irrigation water quality and salinity management
  8. 08Design of water conveyance, control and distribution systems
  9. 09Hydraulics and design of border, check basin and furrow irrigation
  10. 10Design and operation of sprinkler and trickle systems
  11. 11Flow dynamics, drop-size distribution and spray losses in sprinklers
  12. 12Cablegation, surge and bubbler irrigation
  13. 13Automation of irrigation systems
  14. 14Alternative irrigation scheduling and integrated water management
