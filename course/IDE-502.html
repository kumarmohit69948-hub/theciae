Syllabus topic plan

  9. 09Vertical, horizontal subsurface and multiple well-point drainage
  10. 10Drainage materials and filter/envelope design

  12. 12Hooghoudt, Kirkham, Ernst, Glover-Dumm and Kraijenhoff equations
  13. 13Drainage for salinity control; salt balance and leaching requirement
  14. 14Disposal of drainage effluents; reclamation of salt-affected areas
