Ground Water Engineering

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Water resources of India; occurrence and movement of groundwater
  2. 02Principles of groundwater flow
  3. 03Interaction between surface water and groundwater
  4. 04Natural and artificial groundwater recharge
  5. 05Conjunctive use and groundwater balance
  6. 06Water-table fluctuation beneath a recharge site
  7. 07Delineation of groundwater potential zones using RS and GIS; MODFLOW
  8. 08Hydraulics of fully and partially penetrating wells (confined, leaky, unconfined)
  9. 09Flow net analysis and multi-aquifer analysis
  10. 10Flow analysis in interfering wells
  11. 11Pumping tests for aquifer parameter estimation
  12. 12Wells near recharge and impermeable boundaries; skimming wells
  13. 13Design of well fields; saltwater intrusion in inland and coastal aquifers
  14. 14Groundwater models: calibration and validation
