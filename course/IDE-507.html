Remote Sensing and GIS for Land and Water Resource Management

IDE-507 Irrigation & Drainage Engineering

Open notes & files in the library → ▶ Study reels

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Physics of remote sensing; electromagnetic radiation and its interactions
  2. 02Remote sensing platforms: LANDSAT, SPOT, ERS, IKONOS; Indian Space Programme
  3. 03Satellite data analysis and visual interpretation
  4. 04Digital image processing: preprocessing, enhancement, classification, data merging
  5. 05Basic components of GIS; map projections and coordinate systems
  6. 06Spatial data structures: raster and vector; topology and geodatabase models
  7. 07Data quality, sources of error and metadata
  8. 08Thematic mapping and measurements in GIS
  9. 09Query analysis, reclassification, buffering and neighbourhood functions
  10. 10Map overlay, interpolation and network analysis
  11. 11Digital elevation modelling and the Analytical Hierarchy Process
  12. 12Object-oriented and web-based GIS
  13. 13Rainfall-runoff, groundwater and water-quality modelling
  14. 14Flood inundation mapping, drought monitoring and site selection for recharge
