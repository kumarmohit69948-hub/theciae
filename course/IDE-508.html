  9. 09Wastewater treatment: physical, chemical and biological methods
  10. 10Recycling, constructed wetlands and reed-bed systems
  11. 11Carbon footprint of wastewater reuse
  12. 12Environmental standards and CPCB norms
  13. 13Environmental Impact Assessment: stages, monitoring and clearance
