Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Channel characteristics; prismatic and non-prismatic channels
  2. 02Steady, unsteady, uniform and non-uniform flow
  3. 03Energy and momentum; critical flow computation
  4. 04Free-surface flow classification; velocity and pressure distribution
  5. 05Uniform flow, conservation laws and specific energy
  6. 06Channel transitions
  7. 07Gradually, rapidly and spatially varied flow computations
  8. 08Energy dissipation and flow-control structures
  9. 09Flow measurement in channels
  10. 10Design of stable channels; sediment transport and regime theory
  11. 11Tractive force theory
  12. 12Principles of pipe flow and equivalent pipe
  13. 13Pipe network synthesis and analysis; transmission lines
  14. 14Cost considerations for branched and looped systems; system decomposition
