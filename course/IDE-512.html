Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Principles of heat, mass and momentum transport
  2. 02Transport of radiant energy; radiation environment and microclimatology
  3. 03Micrometeorology: turbulent transfer profiles and fluxes
  4. 04Laws of electromagnetic radiation: measurement and estimation
  5. 05Profile balance of heat, mass and momentum in crop communities
  6. 06Climatic change and plant response to environmental stresses
  7. 07Measurement and estimation of potential evapotranspiration
  8. 08Root anatomy, water flow in roots and root-density models
  9. 09Methods of measuring plant water status
  10. 10Estimating ET using the three-temperature model and MODIS
  11. 11Soil-plant-atmosphere system and ET-yield relations
  12. 12Optimal irrigation scheduling using LP and DP; production functions
  13. 13Crop yield modelling and instrumentation for plant environments
