Sensing and Automation in Irrigation Systems

IDE-515 Irrigation & Drainage Engineering

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Sensing and sensors; sensor classifications
  2. 02Wireless sensor networks (WSN): history and communication
  3. 03Design constraints of a WSN: energy, self-management and security
  4. 04Node architecture and the sensing subsystem
  5. 05Analog-to-digital converters
  6. 06Processor subsystem: microcontroller, DSP, ASIC and FPGA
  7. 07Communication interfaces: SPI and I2C
  8. 08Node architectures: IMote, XYZ and Hogthrob
  9. 09Applications in surface irrigation automation
  10. 10Automation based on volume and time
  11. 11Fertigation scheduling automation
  12. 12Monitoring waterlogging, salinity and oxygen diffusion
