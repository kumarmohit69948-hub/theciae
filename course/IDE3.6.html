B.Tech. Agricultural Engineering · Semester 3

Fluid Mechanics and Open Channel Hydraulics

IDE3.6 Irrigation & Drainage Engineering

Open notes & files in the library → ▶ Study reels

Syllabus lecture plan

  1. 01Properties of fluids: ideal and real fluid, units
  2. 02Pressure and its measurement
  3. 03Pascal's law, pressure forces on plane and curved surfaces, centre of 1 pressure, pressure diagram
  4. 04Application of hydrostatics in engineering structures
  5. 05Buoyancy, Archimede's principle, metacentre and metacentric height, 1 condition of floatation and stability of submerged and floating bodies
  6. 06Kinematics of fluid flow: Lagrangian and Eulerian description of fluid 2 motion, continuity equation, path lines, streak lines and stream lines, stream 2 function, velocity potential and flow net
  7. 07Types of fluid flow, translation, rotation, circulation and vorticity, vortex motion
  8. 08Dynamics of fluid flow, Bernoulli's theorem, venturimeter, orifice meter and 2 pitot tube, siphon Flow through orifices (measurement of discharge, 1 measurement of time), flow through mouthpieces; Flow over notches, flow 32 over weirs, end contraction of rectangular weirs, ventilation of weirs, various types of nappe
  9. 10Chezy's formula for loss of head in pipes, flow through simple and compound pipes, transmission of power through pipes
  10. 11Open channel design and hydraulics: Chezy's formula, Bazin's formula
  11. 12Discharge measurement in open channels: current meter
  12. 13Dimensional analysis and similitude: Rayleigh's method and Buckingham's
  13. 14Introduction to fluid machinery
