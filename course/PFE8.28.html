  4. 04Network scheduling: activity, networks, use of CPM, PERT in project 4 scheduling

  6. 06Estimation of project costs, earned value analysis, project techno-economic 4 viability, break-even analysis
  7. 08Government policies for promotion of entrepreneurship in food processing
  8. 09Launching and organizing an enterprise, enterprise selection, market 6 assessment, feasibility study, SWOT analysis
  9. 10Resource mobilization
  10. 11Financial institution in promoting entrepreneurship
  11. 12Supply chain management
  12. 13Case study of a food business
