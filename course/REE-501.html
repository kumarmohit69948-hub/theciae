Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Solar energy: heat transfer, estimation and measurement
  2. 02Solar collectors: FPC, ETC and concentrating collectors
  3. 03Direct and indirect solar energy applications
  4. 04Solar photovoltaic conversion: components and integration
  5. 05Energy from biomass and wastes: production and characterization
  6. 06Biomass conversion: thermochemical, biochemical and agrochemical
  7. 07Wind energy: resource estimation and technologies
  8. 08Wind performance curves; power and torque characteristics
  9. 09Airfoils, rotors and windmill parameters; wind-farm design
  10. 10Ocean thermal, geothermal, tidal and hydro energy
  11. 11Energy conversion systems: integration and analysis
  12. 12Energy storage: types, materials and characteristics
  13. 13Applications of renewable energy systems
