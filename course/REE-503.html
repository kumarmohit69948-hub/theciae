Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Biomass characterization: types, resources and sustainability

  4. 04Biochemical conversion for biogas and bioethanol
  5. 05Biomass combustion: stoichiometric air and chemistry
  6. 06Design of combustion systems; zones, flame stability and emissions
  7. 07Co-firing of biomass
  8. 08Thermochemical conversion: gasification and carbonization
  9. 09Torrefaction and pyrolysis
  10. 10Cogeneration technologies: topping and bottoming cycles
  11. 11Waste heat recovery: estimation, systems and design
