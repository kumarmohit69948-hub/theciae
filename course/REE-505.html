Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Wind mapping and assessment; wind energy potential
  2. 02Nature of wind; Weibull and Rayleigh analysis
  3. 03History, taxonomy and power laws of windmills
  4. 04Wind turbine aerodynamics and momentum theories
  5. 05Airfoils and their characteristics
  6. 06Horizontal-axis wind turbines: blade element and wake analysis
  7. 07Vertical-axis wind turbines: rotor design, power regulation and yaw
  8. 08Site selection; mechanical and electrical applications
  9. 09Wind farms: interfacing and maintenance
  10. 10Stand-alone and grid-connected systems; wind energy storage
  11. 11Wheeling, banking and cost economics
  12. 12Testing and certification; wind turbine loads
  13. 13Wind energy control systems, generators and annual energy output
