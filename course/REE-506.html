Solar Photovoltaic System Design and Analysis

REE-506 Renewable Energy Engineering

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Physics of solar cells: band theory and p-n junctions
  2. 02Absorption, generation, recombination and carrier separation
  3. 03Standard solar cell structure and I-V characteristics
  4. 04Conversion efficiency and losses; effect of radiation and temperature
  5. 05First-generation silicon wafer technology
  6. 06Second-generation thin-film technologies
  7. 07Third-generation and emerging PV technologies
  8. 08Benchmark efficiencies and fabrication technologies
  9. 09SPV systems and Balance of System (BoS)
  10. 10System design with PVSyst: standalone and grid-connected
  11. 11Rooftop business models; canal-top and floating PV
  12. 12Energy storage: batteries, CNTs and ultra-capacitors
