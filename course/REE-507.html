Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Introduction to policy parameters and regulatory bodies

  5. 05Indian energy policy and the Electricity Act

  7. 07Grid code, green corridor, solar and hybrid parks
  8. 08Electricity trading: open access and RPO
  9. 09Distributed generation; international policies and treaties
  10. 10Clean development mechanism and financing of energy systems
  11. 11Project management: cost economics, sensitivity and risk analysis
  12. 12Energy economics: life-cycle costing and investment risk
