Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Introduction to alternate fuels: methanol, ethanol, biogas, producer gas, hydrogen
  2. 02Production, composition and properties of alternate fuels
  3. 03Combustion characteristics and comparison with conventional fuels
  4. 04Fuel cells: principle, classification and efficiency
  5. 05Life-cycle assessment of fuel-cell systems
  6. 06Hydrogen production and gas cleanup
  7. 07Hydrogen storage and the energy economy
  8. 08Thermal and mechanical utilization of alternate fuels
  9. 09Environmental impact and safety factors
  10. 10Efficiency, economics and commercial considerations
