Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Biogas technology: potential, status and chemistry
  2. 02Physical conditions and alternate feedstock materials
  3. 03Reactor types: single-phase and two-phase processes
  4. 04High-rate biomethanation: model and size selection
  5. 05Construction technique and material requirement
  6. 06Design concepts: night-soil, kitchen-waste and cold-condition plants
  7. 07Biogas properties, uses and gas distribution design
  8. 08Utilization devices: scrubbing, compressing and dual-fuel engines
  9. 09Power generation and testing of biogas appliances
  10. 10Effluent handling, treatment and slurry enrichment
  11. 11Cost, financial viability, repair and maintenance
