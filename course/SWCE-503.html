Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Design, planning and layout of conservation structures
  2. 02Criteria for selecting structures by soil, land use and climate
  3. 03Design and construction of earthen dams
  4. 04Stability analysis of land slopes and soil mass, including landslides
  5. 05Hydrological and structural design with stress analysis
  6. 06Hydraulic jump and energy dissipaters
  7. 07Seepage through dams; flow net and uplift pressure in drop structures
  8. 08Design of energy dissipaters
  9. 09Design of water harvesting structures
  10. 10Construction, maintenance and utilization of stored water
  11. 11Mechanized construction techniques for conservation structures
