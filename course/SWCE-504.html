Stochastic Hydrology

SWCE-504 Soil & Water Conservation Engineering

Open notes & files in the library → ▶ Study reels

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Hydrologic cycle, systems concept and hydrologic systems model
  2. 02Classification of hydrologic models: statistical, stochastic, deterministic
  3. 03Statistical characteristics and probability distributions of hydrologic variables
  4. 04Deterministic vs stochastic hydrology; cause-and-effect analysis
  5. 05Hydrologic time series: stationarity, ergodicity and components
  6. 06Parameter estimation of probability distributions
  7. 07Analysis of hydrologic extremes
  8. 08Regression and correlation analysis; prediction equations
  9. 09Stochastic processes and stationary processes
  10. 10Autocorrelation; moving average, AR, ARMA and ARIMA processes
  11. 11Spectral analysis and multivariate hydrologic series
  12. 12Thomas-Fiering and Box-Jenkins models
  13. 13Model formulation: parameter estimation, calibration and validation
  14. 14Data generation, forecasting and regional flood frequency analysis
  15. 15Markov processes and chains; modeling hydrologic uncertainty
