Dryland Water Management Technologies

SWCE-510 Soil & Water Conservation Engineering

Open notes & files in the library → ▶ Study reels

Syllabus topic plan

  1. 01Drought severity assessment: meteorological, hydrological and agricultural methods
  2. 02Drought indices and GIS-based drought information systems
  3. 03Drought vulnerability assessment and mapping using GIS; DPAP programme
  4. 04Types of drought and characterization of environment for water availability
  5. 05Crop planning for erratic and aberrant weather conditions
  6. 06Stress physiology and crop resistance to drought
  7. 07Drought management strategies and crop plans for dryland areas
  8. 08Land shaping and development for soil moisture conservation
  9. 09Soil and moisture conservation for rainfed lands; gel technology
  10. 10Ex-situ measures: water harvesting and micro-catchments
  11. 11Design of small water harvesting structures: farm ponds and percolation tanks
  12. 12Recycling of runoff water and fertility management in dryland farming
  13. 13Watershed planning and development; remote sensing for soil moisture estimation
