Syllabus lecture plan

  1. 01Types of Load and use of BIS code
  2. 02Design of steel structures- specifications, use of IS code (IS 800-2007) and steel 3 table
  3. 03Design of steel sections under tension
  4. 04Design of steel sections under compression
  5. 05Design of steel sections under bending
  6. 06Use of any one design software such as Staad Pro, Etabs, etc. for design of roof 4 truss
  7. 07Design of RCC structures- specifications, use of IS code (IS 456-2000)
  8. 08Analysis and design of singly and doubly reinforced sections
  9. 09Design of beams
  10. 10Design of one way and two way slabs
  11. 11Columns and foundations
  12. 12Design considerations for retaining walls and silos
  13. 13Use of design software for simple RCC structures
