Remote Sensing and GIS Applications

SWCE8.13 Soil & Water Conservation Engineering

Open notes & files in the library → ▶ Study reels

Syllabus lecture plan

  1. 01Basic component of remote sensing (RS), advantages and limitations of RS
  2. 02Possible use of RS techniques in assessment and monitoring of land and 1 water resources
  3. 03Electromagnetic spectrum, energy interactions in the atmosphere and with 2 the Earth's surface
  4. 04Major atmospheric windows, principal applications of different wavelength regions, typical spectral reflectance curve for vegetation, soil and water
  5. 05Different types of sensors and platforms, contrast ratio and possible causes 2 of low contrast
  6. 07Air-photo interpretation- interpretation elements
  7. 08Photogrammetry- measurements on a single vertical aerial photograph, measurements on a stereo-pair- vertical measurement by the parallax 2 method; Ground control for aerial photography
  8. 09Satellite remote sensing, multispectral scanner whiskbroom and push-broom 2 scanner
  9. 10Different types of resolutions
  10. 11Analysis of digital data- image restoration; image enhancement
  11. 12Information extraction, image classification, unsupervised classification, supervised classification, important consideration in the identification of 2 training areas
  12. 13Vegetation indices
  13. 14Microwave remote sensing
  14. 15GIS and basic components
  15. 16Different sources of spatial data, basic spatial entities, major components of 2 spatial data
  16. 17Basic classes of map projections and their properties
  17. 18Methods of data input into GIS, data editing
  18. 19Spatial data models and structures, attribute data management, integrating 1 data (map overlay) in GIS
  19. 20Application of remote sensing and GIS for the management of land and 1 water resources
