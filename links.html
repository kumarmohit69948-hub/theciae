RESOURCE DIRECTORY

Top agricultural & biosystems engineering websites

A hand-picked directory of authoritative resources worldwide — professional societies, leading university departments, research journals, global organizations and free learning — followed by key Indian ICAR institutes, universities and exam bodies. All links open in a new tab.

🌍 Global · Professional societies

ASABE American Society of Agricultural & Biological Engineers — the leading international society, with standards, conferences and journals CIGR International Commission of Agricultural & Biosystems Engineering — the global umbrella body for the discipline EurAgEng European Society of Agricultural Engineers — research network and the AgEng conference series IAgrE Institution of Agricultural Engineers (UK) — professional registration and the journal Landwards CSBE Canadian Society for Bioengineering — Canada's society for agricultural & biosystems engineering

🎓 Global · University departments

Purdue Agricultural & Biological Engineering — one of the top-ranked ABE programs in the US UC Davis Biological & Agricultural Engineering — a leader in agricultural mechanization and food engineering Iowa State Agricultural & Biosystems Engineering — consistently among the highest-ranked ABE departments Cornell Biological & Environmental Engineering — research spanning food, environment and bioprocessing UIUC Agricultural & Biological Engineering, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign Texas A&M Biological & Agricultural Engineering — large program strong in water and machinery Wageningen Wageningen University & Research (Netherlands) — the world's top-ranked agriculture university

📚 Global · Journals & publications

CEA Computers and Electronics in Agriculture — leading journal for AI, sensing and precision agriculture BE Biosystems Engineering — the official research journal of EurAgEng ASABE Library ASABE Technical Library — papers, Transactions of the ASABE and engineering standards

🏛️ Global · Organizations & open data

FAO Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations — global data, standards and publications USDA United States Department of Agriculture — research, statistics and extension resources

📖 Global · Free learning

MIT OCW MIT OpenCourseWare — free lecture notes and courses in core engineering subjects FAO Academy FAO eLearning Academy — free certified courses on agriculture, water and food systems

🇮🇳 India · ICAR & research institutes

ICAR Indian Council of Agricultural Research — the apex body for agricultural research & education in India ICAR-CIAE Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering, Bhopal — the premier ag-engineering research institute ICAR-CIPHET Central Institute of Post-Harvest Engineering & Technology, Ludhiana ICAR-IARI Indian Agricultural Research Institute (Pusa), New Delhi

🏫 India · Universities & departments

IIT Kharagpur Agricultural & Food Engineering Department — the only ag-engineering department among the IITs GBPUAT G.B. Pant University of Agriculture & Technology, Pantnagar — India's first agricultural university PAU Punjab Agricultural University, Ludhiana TNAU Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, Coimbatore TNAU Agritech TNAU Agritech Portal — a large free knowledge base on agriculture & engineering

📝 India · Exams & recruitment

ASRB Agricultural Scientists Recruitment Board — conducts the ICAR-NET, ARS and related exams NTA National Testing Agency — conducts major national entrance and eligibility tests
