OPENINGS DIRECTORY

Where to find JRF, SRF & Post-Doc openings

The official pages where ICAR institutes, IITs, agricultural universities and fellowship schemes announce research positions for agricultural engineering graduates. All links open the institute's own recruitment page in a new tab.

How to use: check these pages 2–3 times a week. Most ICAR project posts (YP-I, YP-II, JRF, SRF) are announced only on the institute's own site, often as walk-in interviews at short notice. Always open the official advertisement for eligibility and the last date.

⚠️ Always verify full details and eligibility on the official website/notification before applying. Deadlines and terms can change — theciae is not responsible for errors or missed deadlines.
